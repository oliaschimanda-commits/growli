About Growli

Farming Information Made Accessible

Who We Are

Growli is a digital farming information platform designed to connect farmers with useful agricultural information, training opportunities and markets.

Our goal is to make farming knowledge easier to access and help farmers make informed decisions that can improve agricultural production.

Our Mission

To provide farmers with accessible, practical and locally relevant agricultural information.

Our Vision

To become a trusted digital platform for farmers seeking knowledge, training and market opportunities.

What Growli Provides

Information

Practical information about farming and agricultural practices.

Training

Training opportunities that help farmers improve their knowledge and skills.

Marketplace

A platform for farmers to promote and sell their agricultural products.
